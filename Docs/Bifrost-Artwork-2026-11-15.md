# Original Bifrost winter artwork

The owner rejected the flat SVG treatment and requested intricate original raster artwork behind frosted glass cards. The two new images were generated with the built-in image tool, without an API key or paid API fallback. No TROA main-site artwork was used. The established frozen TROA logo and favicon remain separate canonical assets.

The desktop scene and portrait companion use frozen black Norse stonework, engraved interlace, silver/glacier ice, restrained old gold, a Bifrost crossing and a distant Asgardian citadel. No interface or logo is baked into the background. Original PNG masters are preserved in the local ignored artwork folder; optimized WebP derivatives are the application assets.

## Final prompts

### Desktop

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original full-bleed raster background for TROA's Bifrost server-management application, landscape 16:9, high resolution, richly detailed cinematic environment art, not a UI mockup. Primary request: an intricate, convincingly material frozen Asgardian Bifrost scene that will remain beautiful behind dark translucent glass interface cards. Scene: an ancient black-stone Norse gateway and monumental carved stone buttresses above a deep frozen fjord; a luminous glacier-ice bridge recedes across the water toward a distant Asgardian citadel. Close foreground stone and layered clear ice show extremely fine frost crystals, ice veins, snow deposited in engraved Nordic interlace, weathered obsidian, silver metal inlays and restrained old-gold fittings. Atmospheric depth: distant snow peaks, sparse stars, cold drifting mist and a very faint pale aurora. Composition: wide cinematic view with the architectural framing and intricate frost detail strongest around the outer edges and lower corners; the middle has dark layered environmental detail and room for readable dashboard overlays, not a blank gradient. Lighting: black/charcoal foundations, silver-white and pale glacier-cyan refraction, tiny restrained warm-gold details. Frosty, noble, quietly powerful TROA/Bifrost/Asgard mood. Keep the overall artwork dark enough for a working operations dashboard while showing real high-frequency material detail. No saturated blue wash, no neon rainbow, no flat vector shapes, no line-diagram style, no CSS-looking gradients or polygon art, no UI panels/cards/buttons drawn into the image, no words, no logos, no fake lettering, no characters, no watermark. Entirely original artwork; do not imitate or reuse existing TROA main-site artwork.

### Mobile companion

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original portrait 9:16 mobile companion background to the supplied Bifrost winter environment, as a coherent second asset in the same art direction, not a UI mockup or a flat crop. The supplied image is a visual style and material reference. Preserve its extraordinarily detailed frost-covered black Norse stonework, interlaced carved arches, silver and tiny aged-gold fittings, convincing ice crystals and glassy frozen stone. Compose a tall view through an ancient carved gateway toward a narrow luminous glacier-ice bridge crossing a misty frozen fjord; an Asgardian citadel rises in the upper distance beneath sparse stars and a restrained pale aurora. Tall intricately engraved frozen pillars frame the edges. Charcoal, silver-white, glacier-cyan refraction, restrained old-gold only; no saturated blue wash. Strong depth and realistic material texture, fine frozen filigree and snow, cinematic quality. Intended behind dark translucent glass dashboard panels on a phone: quieter dark layered detail through the central column, intricate framing readable around the outer edges. It must feel like an original elaborate fantasy environment painting, never CSS/vector/polygon art. No words, no logos, no watermark, no characters, no interface, no cards drawn into the artwork. Do not reuse TROA main-site assets.
